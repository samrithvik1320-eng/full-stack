About Us

  • FSSA is a social-impact initiative by Freshworks (the SaaS company) — it’s designed to give skill-based tech training to students from underserved communities who could not pursue a conventional college degree.
  • The “STS” in the name honours STS Prasad (Siripurapu Taraka Subramanya Prasad), a former SVP of Engineering at Freshworks; the work of the academy was something close to his heart.
  • The programme is free of cost to eligible students.

Joining Process

  • Online Application (basic info)
  • An online aptitude test called FSAT (Freshworks Software Aptitude Test) — covering maths, logical reasoning, English (also Tamil)
  • Interviews (telephonic/video + in-person)
  • Cost to student: Zero. Programme is free.

Qualification

  • Age: roughly 17–20 years (varies slightly by batch)
  • Educational background: 12th pass or 10+ Diploma; not for students who have completed or are deep into a degree course.
  • Household monthly income: Typically less than ₹ 20,000 (in many cases) and priority for government school / Tamil-medium students from rural areas.
