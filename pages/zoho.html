About Us

  • ZSL is an initiative by Zoho Corporation (a global software company headquartered in Chennai) to provide an alternative to the traditional college path by offering practical, career-oriented training.
  • It was launched in 2005 with very few students (6 students & 2 teachers) as a “non-college” route.
  • Over its 20-year journey, ZSL has expanded across multiple locations in Tamil Nadu (for example, new campuses at Tharuvai and Kumbakonam) and multiple “schools” or specialisations: School of Technology, School of Business, School of Design, Graduate Studies, etc.

Joining Process

  • Visit ZSL’s official admission page: there’s an online admission form.
  • Fill in your personal details, academic background, and any required fields.
  • Choose your preferred school/track (Technology, Design, Business, etc.).
  • Submit before the deadline (they announce application windows each year). For example, an earlier posting said last date for a round was “20 January 2022”.

Qualification

  • This program has multiple locations in Tamil Nadu and accepts applicants from across India.
  • It focuses on building a career in areas like software engineering, user experience, and business.
  • Eligibility typically requires candidates to be between 17 and 20 years old and have completed Class 12 or a diploma.
